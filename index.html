Our Value Proposition

We know logistics because we've lived it.

Talent Haven brings dedicated offshore staff solutions for US logistics companies and 3PLs (third-party logistics providers) who require dependable operational support. Unlike a conventional logistics staffing firm, the founders of our logistics staffing company have owned and scaled a 3PL. Our hands-on US 3PL experience enables us to truly understand warehouse management systems, your customer needs, and your team's daily logistics struggles.

Every specialist completes TalentHaven's structured training in 3PL fundamentals before being presented to clients. After selection, your team provides onboarding on your specific SOPs, WMS procedures, customer accounts, and workflows.
